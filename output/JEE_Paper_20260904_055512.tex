\section*{Section 1: Single Correct Choice (Marking: +3, -1)}

\textbf{Q1.} A motorboat going downstream overcame a raft at a point $A$. After time $\tau = 60\text{ min}$, the motorboat turned back and after some time passed the raft at a distance $l = 6.0\text{ km}$ downstream from point $A$. Assuming the duty of the engine to be constant and the river current uniform, find the river flow velocity $v_r$.


\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*), itemsep=2pt]
  \item A) $3.0\text{ km/h}$
  \item B) $6.0\text{ km/h}$
  \item C) $1.5\text{ km/h}$
  \item D) $4.5\text{ km/h}$
\end{enumerate}


\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{ANSWER KEY \& DETAILED SOLUTIONS}}
    \rule{\textwidth}{1pt}
\end{center}

\textbf{Q1. Answer: (A)}\\
In the reference frame of the river water (which moves with velocity $v_r$ relative to the bank):\n1. The raft is at rest.\n2. The motorboat moves away from the raft for time $\tau$ at speed $v_{b/r}$ (boat speed relative to water).\n3. When it turns back, it moves towards the raft with the same relative speed $v_{b/r}$.\n4. Therefore, the boat takes an equal time $\tau$ to return to the raft.\n5. Total time elapsed from point $A$ until meeting the raft again is $T = 2\tau = 2\text{ hours}$.\n6. In this total time $T$, the raft has drifted a distance $l = 6.0\text{ km}$.\n7. Hence, $v_r = \frac{l}{T} = \frac{l}{2\tau} = \frac{6.0\text{ km}}{2\text{ h}} = 3.0\text{ km/h}$.
